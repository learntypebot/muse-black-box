What is the Black Box?

It is a small black box we send to you. It is set up for you, and it is made for your business. You plug it in, and from then on you have your own AI helper — Muse — ready to work for you, right there in your shop or office.

Here is the simple idea behind it. People do not buy a nail. They do not buy the hole in the wall. They just want to hang the picture. The Black Box works the same way. You do not need to know how it works inside. You just say what you want — the picture on the wall — and Muse does the rest.

For Singapore businesses only. The box is sent to you within 7 days after you pay.
